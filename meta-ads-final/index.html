What changed

Your requests, plus a light copy polish.

Step 1 collects the four fields you asked for

Full name, mobile number, email, and "What's happening with your heating system?" as an open text box. The example text shows people what kind of detail helps the tech.

Step 2 collects the full address

Street address, city, postal code, state and country. State is already set to Illinois and country to United States, so most people only type three things.

A progress bar on both steps

"Step 1 of 2" and "Step 2 of 2" tell people how close they are to the end, which makes them more likely to finish.

Copy tightened

The divider now reads "Or request a callback". The form heading is "Tell us what's going on". The step 2 heading is "Where should we send the tech?". The subline now adds that customers hear the price before any work starts.

Our recommendations

Five things that will make it convert better.

1

Save the lead as soon as step 1 is sent

Set the GHL form so the contact is created when someone taps "Continue to step 2", not only at the end. Anyone who leaves at the address step is still a lead your team can call.

2

Pre-fill state and country

Set Illinois and United States as defaults. Every service area is in Illinois, so there's no reason to make people pick them.

3

Consider making email optional

On a phone, email is the field people most often skip or abandon over. Your team calls the customer anyway, so an optional email could bring in more leads. Worth testing once the page is live.

4

Track calls and forms in Meta

Fire the Meta Pixel "Lead" event on the thank-you screen and "Contact" on every tap of the call button, so Meta learns which ads bring real customers. The call button should use the link tel:+17732383838.

5

Confirm the winter offer before launch

The page shows $50 off service calls and up to $1,000 off a new system until November 30. Please confirm the amounts and the end date, and remove the offer block after the 30th.
