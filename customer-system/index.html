7

A thank-you, then a review request Paid

Two separate messages after payment. They are kept apart on purpose.

CS
City & SuburbanText message

Wed 12:15 PM

As a thank-you, here's $50 off your next service with us. Use code THANKS50 any time in the next 12 months.

Thu 10:00 AM

How did Eddie do yesterday? If you have a minute, a Google review helps your neighbors find us: leave a review

The $50 amount is an example. The offer is yours to set.

Jobber Reviews asks every customer for a Google review after payment, and stops asking anyone who has already left one.

Why they are separate: Google doesn't allow discounts in exchange for reviews, and reviews earned that way can be removed. Everyone gets the thank-you, whether or not they review, and everyone is asked for a review, not only happy customers. That keeps your Google rating safe.
